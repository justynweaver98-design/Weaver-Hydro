WHAT WE APPLY

Seed. Mulch.
Water. A plan.

Hydroseeding combines seed, mulch, fertilizer, and water in a slurry applied across prepared soil. The mulch helps hold moisture and supports the start of grass establishment.

Our minimum charge is $500 for up to 2,500 square feet on one property. Tell us your approximate area so we can quote the work.

Bermuda is our default seed choice. Have another seed in mind? Include your request when you contact us so we can discuss your goals and the site.

PLAN WITH CONFIDENCE

Hydroseeding
questions, answered.

Can you hydroseed a new construction property?

Yes. We work with homeowners and builders on lawn establishment after construction. Tell us about the site readiness, approximate area, equipment access, and schedule.

Can hydroseeding help a thin or patchy lawn?

We offer lawn restoration options, but the right approach depends on the existing grass and underlying conditions. Share photos or describe the problem so we can review it.

Do you use Bermuda seed?

Bermuda is our default. Special seed requests are welcome for discussion when you request an estimate.

How long does grass take to establish?

Timing depends on seed, weather, ground condition, watering, and aftercare. We discuss realistic expectations for your project rather than promising a single timeline for every property.

What should I include in my estimate request?

Include your address, approximate square footage or acreage, current ground condition, available water, and preferred timeline. A link to property photos is helpful.
